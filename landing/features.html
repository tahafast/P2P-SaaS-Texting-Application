Platform Features

Every tool your team needs
to run great campaigns

From contact import to post-campaign analytics — TextReach handles the operational complexity so you can focus on your message.

Contact Management
Import, search, filter, and segment your full contact list. Tag contacts, track opt-outs, and view full message history per person.
AI-Powered CSV Import
Drop in any CSV and our AI automatically maps your fields with confidence scores. Deduplication and phone number validation built in.
Campaign Builder
Build campaigns in a guided 5-step wizard. Choose a message template, assign texters, set your sending number, and preview your message live.
Texter Workspace
A clean, distraction-free interface for your texters. One contact at a time, full conversation history, tags, and a single send button. No training required.
Conversation Inbox
A 3-column inbox to manage every inbound reply. Filter by unread, needs reply, or all. Full contact panel and AI reply suggestions included.
Real-Time Analytics
Live delivery rates, reply rates, opt-outs, and texter performance charts. Export any report as CSV. AI weekly operations summary included.
Message Templates
Create and manage a library of message templates with merge fields ({{first_name}}, {{city}}, etc.) and real-time character count.
AI Operations Intelligence
Dashboard-level AI summaries tell you which campaigns are underperforming, which texters need coaching, and what's trending in replies.
Compliance & Audit Log
Full opt-out management, 10DLC compliance routing, and a complete audit log of every action taken by every user on your account.
Texter Workspace

Designed for volunteers,
not just power users

Your texters don't need to be tech-savvy. The workspace shows one contact at a time — read the pre-written message, personalize if you want, hit send. That's it. Progress bars and live reply counts keep motivation high.

  • Zero training required — new texters are live in minutes
  • Full conversation history shown per contact
  • Tags, notes, and opt-out controls in one panel
  • Live progress counter — contacts sent vs remaining
