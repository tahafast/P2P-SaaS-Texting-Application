FAQ

Pricing questions answered

Is there a free trial?+
Yes — all plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You get full access to all features on your chosen plan during the trial period.
What happens if I go over my monthly message limit?+
Overage messages are billed automatically at $0.007/message on Starter and $0.005/message on Growth. We'll notify you at 80% and 100% of your limit. Enterprise plans have custom volume agreements.
Can I switch plans mid-cycle?+
Yes — you can upgrade instantly at any time. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Prorated credit is applied when upgrading.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?+
Yes. Registered 501(c)(3) organizations receive 15% off any plan. Contact our sales team with your EIN and we'll apply the discount to your account.
What's the annual billing discount?+
Annual billing gives you 2 months free — equivalent to a 20% discount vs. monthly billing. You're billed once per year and can cancel before renewal.
